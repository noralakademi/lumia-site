With you through menopause

We built Lumia to support you through perimenopause and menopause. To track your symptoms, learn, find support and get to know your own body better — we’re coming soon to the App Store.

  • Symptom journal Record hot flashes, sleep, mood and more in a few taps.
  • Cycle tracking Follow your cycle day and periods.
  • Regimen tracking Record the supplements and medicines you take, with their times, and keep your intake marks.
  • Pattern insight As your records build up, see what rises and when.
  • Sleep program A 7-day program and sleep diary grounded in the principles of cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I).
  • Calming tools Paced breathing, 5-4-3-2-1 grounding and a guided body scan.
