Privacy Policy - Lumia

A summary in a few sentences

Your health records stay on your phone. Your symptom, cycle, medication, sleep and stage records are not sent to our servers. The server holds only your account (your email address, your nickname, your session and security logs, and a copy of your language preference) and your consent record.

In this text, "Lumia" or "we" means the company above; "the App" means the Lumia mobile application.

Lumia is a tracking and information app for people in menopause and perimenopause. It is not a medical device; it does not diagnose, and it does not recommend medication or dosage.

2. What data do we process?

2.1 Data that stays on your phone (never sent to us)

All of this lives in a local database file on your device, and we have no access to it:

  • Your symptom records (hot flashes, sleep, mood, and so on)
  • Your cycle records
  • Your hormone therapy and medication records, and intake marks
  • Your sleep diary and sleep programme progress
  • Your reminders
  • Your menopause stage and your answers to the stage assessment
  • Which articles you have read
  • Your app settings (breathing exercise duration, vibration, and so on)

Usage measurement sends none of the records in this list (§4.1).

The one exception is your own setting: if your phone's iCloud backup is on, the operating system backs this file up too. That backup sits in Apple's infrastructure and is outside our control.

2.2 Data held on the server

  • Your account: email address, a cryptographic hash of your password, account creation and last sign-in timestamps. (You can also use the app anonymously, without an account; in that case there is only an anonymous identifier assigned to your device.)
  • Your consent record: which consent you gave, against which version of the text, when; and, if you withdrew it, the withdrawal record. We keep this so we can prove that consent was given and withdrawn.
  • Your nickname (optional).
  • A copy of your language preference (tr or en). Your choice is kept on your device and the app reads it from there; the copy on the server exists only so that we can send the verification and password-reset emails in your language.
  • Infrastructure logs: session and security records.

2.3 Data we do NOT process

  • No advertising identifier, no ad networks.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking (§4.1).
  • No location data.
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No sale to data brokers; we do not sell, rent or share any data for advertising.
  • We do not ask for your name or a profile photo.

4.1 Usage measurement

To be able to improve the App, we measure how you use it: which screen and tool you opened, where you are in the setup steps, which area a record was created in, and where the App hit an error.

Your symptom, cycle and medication records do not take part in this measurement; neither do your sleep diary, your notes or your menopause stage. The content of a record is never sent. Which article you read is not sent either.

The measurement uses a random identifier generated on your device and is not combined with your account. It is not required: it runs only if you consent, no feature of the App depends on it, and you can turn it off at any time under Profile → Privacy and data → Consent preferences → Usage statistics.

The measurement is carried out on our behalf by PostHog, Inc. (US); the data is stored in Frankfurt but processed from countries outside Europe, including the US, and is kept by the provider for a limited period. The basis for this is your explicit consent. Details are in §5.1 of the Privacy Notice.

5. Where is your data processed?

Our servers are currently in Frankfurt, Germany. This means the data in §2.2 is processed outside Türkiye.

Your health records (§2.1) stay on your phone; they are not sent to our servers.

The fact that "this email address is registered with Lumia" can itself amount to a health inference in context; that is why the transferred set is deliberately kept minimal. The legal basis for this limited transfer is your explicit consent (asked as a separate item on the consent screen); the details are in §7 of the Privacy Notice.

6. Backups and exporting your data

From the Export my data screen you can create an encrypted backup of the records on your device.

  • You choose the password, and it stays only with you.
  • Our server sends the file to your verified email address.
  • We cannot decrypt it; we do not hold the key.
  • We keep no permanent copy.

Keeping the backup file in your own mailbox is your responsibility. Do not share the password through the same channel as the file.

9. How is your data protected?

  • All communication with the server uses an encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Every record on the server is protected by row-level access rules: each user can reach only their own data.
  • Your password is never stored in plain text (cryptographic hash).
  • Your backup file is encrypted with the password you choose; we do not hold the key.

An honest limit: the local database file on your device is not separately encrypted; it is protected by your phone's own screen lock and disk encryption. We recommend keeping your phone locked.

10. Children

Lumia is not designed for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn that we have processed a child's data, we delete the account and records on the server (we have no remote access to the records on the device).

By using the App and when you create an account, you confirm that you are at least 18 (Terms of Use §1); our store age rating reflects this. We do not ask for an identity document or a date of birth; doing so would mean collecting more personal data about you in order to verify your age.

11. If this policy changes

When we update this text we change the effective date and announce significant changes inside the app. If a change broadens the scope of health-data processing, we ask for fresh explicit consent.
