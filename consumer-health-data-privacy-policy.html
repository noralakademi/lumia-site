Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy - Lumia

Effective date: 18 November 2026

This policy applies to you if you live in the US state of Washington or Nevada, or if your consumer health data is collected in Washington. It is published under Washington's My Health My Data Act and Nevada's consumer health data law.

Lumia is provided by Neural Academy Ltd. Şti., Çukurambar mah. Zeki Ergezen cad. 2/4, Çankaya/Ankara, Türkiye. Contact: destek@lumiaapp.net.

1. Consumer health data we collect, and how we use it

Lumia is an app for menopause and perimenopause, so we treat all of the data below as consumer health data.

Data Why we collect it and how we use it
Records you enter in the App: symptoms, cycle, hormone therapy and medication records with intake marks, sleep diary and sleep programme progress, reminders, your menopause stage and stage assessment answers, which articles you have read To provide the App's features to you: tracking, reminders and content. These records are processed only on your device. They are not sent to us and we cannot access them.
Your account: email address, password hash, account creation and last sign-in times, optional nickname, a copy of your language preference To create your account, sign you in, and send verification, password-reset and backup emails in your language. Because Lumia is a menopause app, having an account with us can itself reveal information about your health.
Your consent record: which consents you gave or withdrew, for which version of the text, and when To be able to prove that you gave consent, and when you withdrew it.
Session and security logs To keep your account secure and prevent abuse.
Your encrypted backup file, only if you ask for a backup To deliver the backup to your own email address. It passes through our server and our email provider. It is encrypted with a password only you know, we cannot read it, and we keep no copy.
Usage measurement, only if you consent: which screens and tools you open (for example symptom entry, hormone therapy or the sleep programme), your step in the setup, the area in which you created a record (for example symptom or medication, never its content), and errors. It is linked to a random identifier created on your device, not to your account. To understand how the App is used and to improve it. No feature of the App depends on it.

2. Where this data comes from

  • From you, when you use the App or create an account.
  • From your device, automatically: session and security logs, and, only if you consent, usage measurement.

3. Consumer health data we share, and with whom

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it for advertising.

We share consumer health data with no third party except government authorities, and only when we are legally required to respond to a lawful request; we then disclose only the data that the request lawfully covers. We have no affiliates.

The following service providers process data on our behalf, under a contract and only for the purposes in §1:

Service provider What it does for us Data it processes
Supabase Database, sign-in, server functions Your account, consent record, session and security logs, and your encrypted backup while it is being sent
Brevo Email delivery Your email address; for backups, also the email subject and the encrypted backup file
PostHog, Inc. Usage measurement Usage measurement data, only if you consent

No third party collects consumer health data about your activities across different apps or websites over time through Lumia.

4. Your rights and how to use them

You have the right to:

  • Know and access: confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, get a copy of it, and get a list of every third party and affiliate we have shared it with, with a way to contact them. The records that stay on your device can be exported directly from Profile → Privacy and data → Export my data.
  • Withdraw consent: at any time, in Profile → Privacy and data → Consent preferences. Usage measurement can be turned off on its own under Usage statistics on the same screen.
  • Delete: Profile → Privacy and data → Delete account deletes your account and the records on your device. We also delete your data from our records, ask our service providers to delete it, and remove it from any backups within six months. After deletion we keep only a record that consent was given or withdrawn, linked to a random account number and not to your email address, for 5 years as proof of consent; it contains no health records.
  • Review and ask for changes to the data we hold about you.

For anything you cannot do in the App, write to destek@lumiaapp.net. If you have an account, write from its email address so that we can verify the request. Usage measurement records are linked only to a random identifier on your device, not to your account or email address; to find them we will ask you for information that lets us locate them, and if we cannot locate them we will tell you.

We respond within 45 days. If we need more time, we may extend this once by another 45 days, and we will tell you within the first 45 days. Requests are free of charge, up to twice in a 12-month period.

Appeal: if we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject line. We will respond in writing within 45 days and explain our decision. If we deny your appeal and you live in Washington, you can contact the Washington State Attorney General: atg.wa.gov/file-complaint.

5. If this policy changes

We will update the effective date above and announce significant changes inside the App. We will not collect new categories of consumer health data, or use or share it for new purposes, without telling you first and getting your consent.
